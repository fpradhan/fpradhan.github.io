{%- comment -%} iOS Safari scans text for dates, phone numbers and addresses and wraps what it finds in its own links, styled as links and absent from the markup. On the CV it turned "11/2024 – 11/2025" into a half-underlined red date link. This asks it not to; assets/css/style.css carries a backstop for versions that do it anyway. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Cache-busting stamp. The stylesheet lives at a fixed path, so a browser that cached an earlier version will keep serving it even after the HTML changes — which is exactly what happened when the site was rebuilt: new markup was rendered against the previous stylesheet, so `.container` and `.submenu` had no rules and the page lost its width and its dropdown. Appending the build time makes every deploy a new URL, so that cannot recur. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign v = site.time | date: '%Y%m%d%H%M%S' -%} {%- comment -%} jekyll-seo-tag writes , description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card and JSON-LD from _config.yml and the page front matter.
