## Article Critique #1 — Mandatory Hepatology Education for Internal Medicine Residents

**Course:** HPTT 830 – Research Analysis and Design for Health Professions Education (Spring 2026)
{: .meta}

### Description

A methodological appraisal of research examining mandatory hepatology education for internal
medicine residents — evidence that sits directly beneath the premise this portfolio has argued
since 2023, that hepatology is underrepresented in medical training.

### Reflection
