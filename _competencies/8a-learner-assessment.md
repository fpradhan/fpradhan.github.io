## Test Blueprint and Psychometric Item Analysis

**Course:** HPTT 805 – Evaluation and Assessment of Teaching and Learning in Health Professions Education (Fall 2024)
{: .meta}

### Description

A paired artifact: designing a defensible assessment, then checking whether it worked. The
blueprint specifies a 25-item multiple-choice and six short-answer summative examination across
three hepatology content domains, mapped against three Bloom levels — knowledge 35%,
comprehension 30%, application 35% — for 50 points total. The item analysis then works through
difficulty index, discrimination index, and Q-index per item, distinguishes competency items
from discrimination items with their different target ranges, recommends specific remedies such
as revising non-functioning distractors versus revising the stem, and interprets what a falling
KR-20 across repeat administrations would signify.

## Endoscopic Skills Blueprint Rubric

**Course:** HPTT 805 – Evaluation and Assessment of Teaching and Learning in Health Professions Education (Fall 2024)
{: .meta}

### Description

A five-point anchored rubric assessing gastroenterology fellows' upper endoscopy and
colonoscopy performance across cognitive and technical domains, scored by attending physicians.
It is designed for serial use across the three years of fellowship, with the explicit
expectation that first-year scores will be low and rise — and recommends conducting assessments
on procedures where a therapeutic intervention is anticipated, for a more complete evaluation.
