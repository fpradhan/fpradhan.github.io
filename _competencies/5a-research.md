## Research Proposal — “AI-Augmented Gamification as a Tool for Interactive Medical Student Education”

**Course:** HPTT 830 – Research Analysis and Design for Health Professions Education (Spring 2026)
{: .meta}

### Description

A complete research proposal moving from problem statement through literature to methodology.
It sets the workforce case directly — a projected 35% shortage of adult transplant
hepatologists by 2033 and 62% of transplant hepatology fellowship positions unfilled in 2024 —
alongside the undergraduate exposure gap, and hypothesises a role for AI personalisation in
narrative-based educational games given Generation Z learners' documented need for customised
feedback.
