## Introduction to Steatotic Liver Disease e-Module

**Course:** HPTT 825 – Essentials of E-Module Development (Summer 2024)
{: .meta}

### Reflection

This assignment was one of the more challenging tasks throughout the MHPTT program, but overall,
it was the most rewarding. The work throughout the HPTT 825 course culminated in the development
of an e-Learning module using Articulate Storyline and I am quite proud of the final product. I
selected this artifact to represent Innovation in Teaching as it embraces interactivity to engage
medical students while augmenting their classroom lectures on the topic.

Some of the challenges encountered included gaining software familiarity while adhering to
e-module best practices, as well as the timespan to complete with this being a summer semester
course. However, challenges aside, completing this course encouraged me to apply for the
eLearning funded awards program, and motivated two medical students to create their own eLearning
modules. The work through this and the subsequent modules I have created have garnered interest
with national hepatology societies to help create online asynchronous educational content
specific to medical students and trainees.

I aim to take the experience gained from this course to continue working with national medical
societies, such as the AASLD, to develop online asynchronous interactive content for medical
students and trainees as well as to expand the work being done in my Enhanced Medical Education
track to make this a regional opportunity.
