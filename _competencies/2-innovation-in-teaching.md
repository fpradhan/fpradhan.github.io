## “Introduction to Steatotic Liver Disease” — Interactive E-Module

**Course:** HPTT 825 – Essentials of E-Module Development (Summer 2024)
{: .meta}

### Description

A 15-minute interactive Articulate Storyline module opening on a clinical case as a narrative
hook, covering the sub-types of steatotic liver disease with a focus on MASLD and its
therapeutic and lifestyle interventions. Navigation is branched so learners can skip to a
section for review, and formative questions are interspersed throughout with tailored correct,
incorrect, and hint feedback. The module was peer-reviewed twice against the Nebraska
E-Learning Scorecard (NEscore) and is accompanied by a full storyboard, module integration
plan, and narration transcript.

## Acute Liver Failure Escape Room Simulation

**Course:** HPTT 824 – Simulated and Virtual Learning in Health Professions Education (Fall 2025)
{: .meta}

### Description

A team-based escape-room simulation for acute liver failure, documented through a full
Simulation Scenario Builder covering prebriefing, learning objectives, scenario flow,
debriefing, and evaluation. The format is inherently collaborative and interdisciplinary, using
time pressure and sequential problem-solving to teach recognition and management of a condition
where decisions are consequential and fast.
