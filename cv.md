## Research and clinical interests

- Hepatology education in undergraduate and graduate medical curricula, and the transplant hepatology workforce
- Point-of-care ultrasound, sarcopenia and frailty in cirrhosis and transplantation
- Artificial intelligence in patient education and in medical education
- Nutrition and micronutrient deficiency in the liver transplant pathway
- Intestinal failure, short bowel syndrome and intestinal transplantation

**Clinical interests** — chronic liver disease, cirrhosis, liver transplantation, short bowel
syndrome and intestinal transplantation, and medical education.
